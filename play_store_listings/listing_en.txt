Al Quran (القرآن الكريم) is your complete Holy Quran app designed to elevate your daily Islamic practice. Read, listen, and study the Noble Quran offline with authentic translations, Tafsir commentaries, word-by-word analysis, smart Quran search, MP3 audio, prayer times, Azan alarm, Home & Lock Screen Widgets, Qibla compass, and Multi-Device Cloud Sync.

Recite the Quran offline, glance at daily Ayahs and prayer times from your home screen, backup and sync notes across devices, and master recitation with Tajweed rules.
