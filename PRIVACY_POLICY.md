# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 11, 2026

**Quran's Tafsir, Audio and Prayer** ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. No Personal Data Collection
We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users. 
- **No Account Required**: You can use all features of the App without creating an account.
- **No Analytics**: We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics).
- **No Data Transmission**: Once the App is set up, it is fully functional offline. No user data is ever transmitted to our servers or any third party.

## 2. App Permissions and Their Use
The App requests certain permissions to provide its core features. All data processed via these permissions stays on your device:

- **Location (GPS)**: Used exclusively to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for your current location. This data is processed locally and is never uploaded.
- **Internet Access**: Used only to download Quranic text, translations, tafsirs, and audio files upon your request.
- **Notifications & Alarms**: Used to provide Adhan alerts and prayer time reminders.
- **Foreground Service**: Used to allow continuous audio recitation playback when the App is in the background.
- **Storage/Media**: Used to save downloaded Quranic resources and audio files on your device for offline use.

## 3. Third-Party Services
If you choose to use features that fetch data from third-party APIs (such as the Quran Foundation API for content updates), your request will be subject to their respective privacy policies. However, the App itself does not share your personal identity with these services.

## 4. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
